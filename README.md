IGB100Project-Grass---Go Touch Grass!
----------------------------
Timmy has been playing too many games lately and was suddenly sucked into hell! Help Timmy kill demons and feed the mysterious patch of Grass their souls.

The Grass promises freedom, just touch it.

Gameplay
-----------------------
Go Touch Grass! is a SHMUP where the main objective is to kill 500 demons and once their souls have been fed to the Grass, touch the Grass to win.
This game is not wave based and enemies will spawn constantly.
